Your privacy matters

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TrendyBot 2.0 and Trendy Core handle information when you use our website, account services, WhatsApp bot and dashboard. We aim to collect only information needed to operate, protect and improve the service.

1. Information associated with website accounts

Depending on how you register and use the service, information may include:

  • Your email address and account display information.
  • A securely hashed password rather than your plain-text password.
  • Account verification and password-reset records. Authentication tokens are stored as hashes where implemented.
  • Login-session records and security information needed to authenticate requests.
  • For Google sign-in, account information and identifiers needed to authenticate and link your Google account.

2. WhatsApp identifiers and message processing

When you interact with TrendyBot or connect a WhatsApp session, the bot may process WhatsApp identifiers such as JIDs. These identifiers help the system identify accounts, manage sessions, apply features and respond to commands. Depending on the feature and technical operation, JIDs may be handled or retained by the bot or its supporting systems.

TrendyBot processes WhatsApp messages and related information as necessary to provide its services, execute user-requested commands, respond to interactions and operate features enabled by users. This processing helps the bot understand requests, carry out commands and deliver the functions users choose to access.

Depending on the features being used, the information processed may include message text, media, sender and chat identifiers, and other information required for the requested function. Certain features may handle message-related information as part of their normal operation.

TrendyBot is designed to automate bot functions and provide users with convenient tools. It is not intended to operate as a general-purpose archive of users' private WhatsApp conversations. We aim to limit the processing of message information to what is reasonably necessary to provide, maintain and protect the service, and do not use private conversations for unrelated purposes.

Users should connect only WhatsApp accounts they own or are authorised to manage. By connecting an account and using TrendyBot, users understand that WhatsApp activity may be processed to carry out the commands and features they choose to use.

WhatsApp is a third-party platform with its own terms and privacy practices. TrendyBot does not control how WhatsApp independently collects or handles information on its platform. Users should also review WhatsApp's applicable privacy policy and terms.

The dashboard is designed to display operational information, such as command names and timestamps, rather than private message previews, contact names or phone numbers.

3. How we use information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and secure accounts and authenticate users.
  • Verify email addresses and process password-reset requests.
  • Connect and manage WhatsApp sessions and dashboard features.
  • Process commands and provide requested bot functions.
  • Protect the service, investigate abuse, troubleshoot errors and maintain reliability.
  • Meet legal obligations and respond to valid legal requests.

4. Browser storage

The website may use browser storage, including sessionStorage or localStorage, to maintain login and WhatsApp-session access information so that you can use the dashboard. Anyone with access to your unlocked device or browser profile may be able to access information stored there. Use a device you control, protect it with a screen lock and sign out when appropriate.

5. Service providers and sharing

We may rely on infrastructure and service providers to operate the website, database, authentication, email delivery, security checks and network connectivity. Information may be processed by those providers only as needed to provide, secure or support the service, subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices.

We do not sell personal information. We do not disclose personal information to unrelated parties for their own marketing. Information may be disclosed where required by law, to protect users or the service, or as part of a legitimate business or service transfer with appropriate safeguards.

6. Retention

Information is kept for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including account operation, security, troubleshooting and legal obligations. Different records may have different retention periods. We have not stated a fixed retention period for WhatsApp JIDs or operational logs because those periods depend on the relevant feature and system configuration.

7. Security

We use technical and organizational measures intended to protect account and service information. No online service, device, transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your password, verification links, access tokens, WhatsApp pairing codes and QR codes private. Never send them to someone claiming to provide support.

8. Your choices and requests

You can stop using TrendyBot and may request access, correction or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable law and legitimate security or legal retention needs. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. Deleting an account or disconnecting a WhatsApp session may not automatically erase every backup or record held by a service provider.

For a privacy request, contact TrendyBot through its official support channel. Before publication, the operator should add a verified support email address or contact link here and confirm the account-deletion process.

9. Children's privacy

TrendyBot is not intended for children who are not legally permitted to use the service or enter into the relevant agreement. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us through the official support channel.

10. International processing

Our hosting and service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where applicable, we will take reasonable steps to ensure that such processing is handled in accordance with relevant data-protection requirements.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy when our features, providers or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published on this page with its update date.

12. Contact

For questions or requests about privacy, contact TrendyBot through its official support channel. A verified support email address or contact link must be added before this Policy is published as final.

This Policy describes intended handling at a general level. Actual data collection, retention and deletion practices should be checked against the deployed code and service-provider settings before final publication.
